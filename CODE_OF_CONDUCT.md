# Community Code of Conduct

Wattwise welcomes people of all backgrounds and experience levels. Our goal is a useful, respectful space for learning and improving the project.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people. Explain disagreements with evidence.
- Respect privacy, accessibility needs and different levels of technical experience.
- Accept feedback and acknowledge uncertainty—especially around measurements and hardware support.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, hate speech, discriminatory language, sexual harassment, threats, deliberate disruption, spam, and publishing another person's private information are not welcome.

## Moderation and reporting

The repository maintainer may edit or remove content, close disruptive threads, or restrict participation to protect the community. Actions should be proportionate to the behavior and its impact.

For public violations, flag the relevant content using GitHub's reporting tools. If a report needs privacy, request a private contact channel without posting personal information or sensitive details. Do not publicly identify affected people without their consent. GitHub's own abuse-reporting mechanisms remain available.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests and project-hosted discussions. It is a project policy, not a promise of round-the-clock moderation.
